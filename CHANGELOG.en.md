## 2026.11.3.1 — 2026-11-25

- Sliding compatibility window: minimum release is the validated Home Assistant month minus three calendar months; prerequisites and tests stay synchronized automatically.
- Validate this minimum before every automatic publication.
- All families move to **2026.11.3.1**.
- The dispatcher records deliveries accepted by a channel and also works when Logbook is disabled.
- Battery Watch retries failed immediate alerts on the next scan.
- Critical speech uses clip volume on supported Sonos speakers and the current volume on other speakers.
